# Producer projection interface 015

STATUS: implementation candidate under the current request. No law promotion, authority seal, world commit, repository commit, or push.

## What this implements

Three role-specific native emitters consume producer-owned AUTHOR declarations and contacts. A contact reaches a seed only when its producer, source-record identity, domain identity, declared coordinate, and verdict type match. The emitted seed retains its content reference separately from its verdict.

`DOMAIN` is not inferred from emitted payloads. `SUPPORTED` and `UNSUPPORTED` are explicit declaration states; the latter emits PENDING, not the verdict NO. A missing declaration or contact does not supply an exclusion. Shared positions are the supported-domain intersection. Private positions require an explicit unsupported declaration from the other producer. Both absent references on a shared position remain a shared-domain MAYBE, never a private position.

Every pair-relative result carries the full pair context, including both producer identities. Domain contradiction emits `P15_DOMAIN_CONFLICT`; the positive closure does not retract already derived facts. This component is not an authorization boundary or a general declaration-schema validator. No contradictory input is claimed to license a seal or EXECUTE.

## Verdicts and references

`source/r4_verdicts.mal` realizes the already-bound identity derivations and quaternary meet. WHERE and WHICH receive WHAT's verdict, WHEN receives HOW's verdict, and WHENCE receives the five-way meet including that producer's FOR-WHAT(BECAUSE). References are not normalized, copied into derived positions, or replaced by verdicts.

Partial inputs produce partial R1/R2 closure. They do not claim a complete R4 normal form. A complete 45-cell fixture separately checks 60 derived coordinates. The scalar/table fixtures qualify the functions, not a world section.

The unchanged 014 content comparator receives only shared positions with supplied seed references. Explicit reference absence yields MAYBE without a wall. A missing contact yields no comparison, not a private position. Unequal content remains on the shared position and produces a wall.
